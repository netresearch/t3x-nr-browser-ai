 The request row stays hidden until the bundle reports a usable model. The form below it does not: it is the plugin's actual content and has to be operable by hand on every browser, with or without an assistant.

The answer in words, directly under the request that asked for it. Rendered from untrusted model output through the same safe DOM construction the page assistant uses — never innerHTML.

Open until a run fills it. Collapsing afterwards puts the answer next to the question instead of below seventy controls, and the disclosure keeps the derived parameters one keystroke away — which is the whole point of writing them into the visible form rather than querying behind its back.
Deliberately outside the assistant block: what the tool exposes and what the model is told is worth reading whether or not this browser can run a model. Every value is escaped by Fluid.
